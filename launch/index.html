Trading signals: your questions answered

What does an Artemis Signals trading signal include?

Structured setups include a market, direction, timeframe, entry level, stop loss and up to three take-profit levels (TP1, TP2 and TP3). Full details are reserved for eligible subscribers; public previews are delayed.

Which markets and timeframes are covered?

The platform covers configured Forex pairs, gold and other metals, indices and crypto instruments. Coverage depends on your subscription and enabled scanners. The example signal cards illustrate M15, M30 and H1 setups; availability can change during early access.

How are signals delivered?

Eligible subscribers review signals and lifecycle history in the dashboard. Private Telegram delivery is live for selected linked ELITE accounts. Delivery requires account validation and connection, and follows saved alert preferences. Joining the waitlist does not activate alerts or a subscription.

What is Artemis Intelligence?

Artemis Intelligence is our planned ELITE AI feature set: market briefings, setup explanations, and explanations of recorded strategy statistics. It is in development and not enabled yet. Predictive ML assessments are a separate research possibility and are not an available product feature.

What do TP1, TP2 and TP3 mean?

They are the first, second and third take-profit levels associated with a setup. A recorded hit means the platform logged a target event. It does not prove that a subscriber entered, executed or profited from a trade.

Are the public preview and performance figures live?

Live Pulse uses a delayed public activity feed when connected. The performance section labels its data as a dated snapshot or a periodically refreshed aggregate feed and shows the source update time. Neither provides an execution feed.

How do I request early access or a trial?

Join the early-access list and confirm your email. A five-day PRO or ELITE trial may be granted manually, subject to availability. Joining does not start a trial, and no payment is needed to join the list.

Do the signals guarantee profits?

No. Trading involves the risk of losing capital. Target achievement is not a win rate or realized return, and past records do not guarantee future results.
