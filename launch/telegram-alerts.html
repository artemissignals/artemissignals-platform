Artemis Signals Telegram alerts

Follow new setups and recorded signal milestones in a private Telegram chat. Delivery is live for selected linked ELITE accounts; wider availability will follow.

Connect your account in the dashboard

  1. Open the Telegram connection section. Sign in to the subscriber dashboard and check whether linking is available for your account.
  2. Create your personal link. Use the single-use link provided by the dashboard to open Telegram and start the bot.
  3. Confirm the account. Return to the dashboard and check the Telegram identity before completing the link.
  4. Check eligibility and connection. Account validation and Telegram connection are separate requirements. Both must be valid for enabled delivery.

An ELITE account or a connected Telegram chat alone does not guarantee delivery. Your account must be included in the enabled rollout, validated, and linked successfully.

Choose the alerts that matter to you

Match your preferences

For enabled accounts, saved market, instrument, timeframe, direction, and minimum-quality preferences control matching new alerts.

A quality score describes signal criteria. It is not a probability of profit.

Follow recorded milestones

Messages can cover new setups and recorded target or stop updates. Open the dashboard to review the complete setup and its lifecycle.

Newly enabled delivery does not replay all historical signals.

Read a stop update in context

A stop can be recorded after TP1 or another target. Review the target count and event wording rather than treating every stop message as a complete loss. Recorded milestones describe platform observations, not your personal trading returns.

If an alert does not arrive

  • Check that your account is validated and included in the enabled ELITE rollout.
  • Check that the expected Telegram account is connected in the dashboard.
  • Review your saved preferences and minimum quality threshold.
  • Check whether a new matching signal or event has been published.
  • Check Telegram notification settings, muted chats, and device notification controls if messages arrive without a sound.

If the problem continues, contact support@artemissignals.ai. Never share your password or authentication tokens.

Frequently asked questions

Does joining early access enable Telegram alerts?

No. Joining the list requests early access. Delivery requires an eligible enabled account and a validated Telegram link.

Are these trading instructions executed automatically?

No. Messages communicate published signals and recorded updates. They do not execute trades or confirm an execution in your account.

Can every ELITE account receive alerts now?

No. Telegram delivery currently has a limited rollout for selected linked ELITE accounts.

Where can I see the full signal history?

Use the subscriber dashboard to review available signal details and recorded lifecycle history. Read the signal guide for an explanation of the fields.
